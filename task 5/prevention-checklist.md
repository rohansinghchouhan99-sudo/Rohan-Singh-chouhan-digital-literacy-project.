## General Online Safety

1. **Check the URL before entering any credentials** – Always look at the browser address bar. Legitimate sites use HTTPS and the correct domain. A site like "vitbhopal-notifications.in" is NOT the same as "vitbhopal.ac.in".

2. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** – Turn on 2FA for your email, social media, and any academic portal that supports it. Even if your password is stolen, 2FA prevents the attacker from logging in.

3. **Never click links in unexpected emails or SMS messages** – Type important URLs directly into your browser. If an email claims to be from your bank or university, navigate to the official website manually.

4. **Use a strong, unique password for every account** – A strong password has at least 12 characters and mixes letters, numbers, and symbols. Use a free password manager (like Bitwarden) to remember them.

5. **Keep your devices and apps updated** – Software updates often include critical security patches. Enable automatic updates on your phone and laptop.

6. **Do not use public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks** – Avoid logging into banking apps, university portals, or email accounts over public Wi-Fi in cafes, airports, or hostels unless you use a VPN.

7. **Log out of accounts on shared computers** – Always log out of every account after using college lab computers or a friend's device.

8. **Be sceptical of offers that seem too good to be true** – Free iPhone giveaways, scholarship scams, and "you have won a prize" messages are almost always phishing attempts.

---

## Financial & UPI Safety (Mandatory for Indian Students)

9. **Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or bank password with anyone** – No legitimate bank, UPI app, or government office will ever ask for these. Not even on a phone call claiming to be from your bank.

10. **Verify the receiver's UPI ID before sending money** – Double-check the UPI ID and the name displayed before confirming any transfer, especially to new contacts. Once sent, UPI payments cannot be reversed.

## How to Report Cybercrime in India

| Channel | Details |
|---------|---------|
| National Cyber Crime Portal | cybercrime.gov.in |
| National Helpline | **1930** (24x7, toll-free) |
| Local Police | File an FIR at your nearest police station |
| Your Bank | Call the bank's fraud helpline to freeze the account immediately |

**Important:** Report as quickly as possible. In UPI fraud cases, funds can sometimes be recovered if reported within the first few hours.
