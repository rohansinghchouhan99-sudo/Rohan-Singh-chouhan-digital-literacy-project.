# Case Study: Phishing Attack on a College Student
## Digital Literacy Project – Task 5 | CSE0001 | VIT Bhopal University

---

## Overview

**Cybercrime Type:** Phishing
**Target Profile:** First-year engineering student, Arjun Mehta (fictional scenario)
**Platform:** Email + Spoofed University Portal

---

## How It Happened – Step by Step

**Step 1 – The Lure:**
Arjun receives an urgent email from what appears to be his university's examination portal (exam-portal@vitbhopal-notifications.in). The email warns that his semester exam registration is incomplete and will be cancelled in 24 hours unless he logs in immediately to verify his details.

**Step 2 – The Hook:**
The email contains a "Verify Now" button linking to http://vitbhopal-examportal.net — a domain that looks legitimate at a glance but is not the official university website. The page is a pixel-perfect clone of the real portal.

**Step 3 – Credential Harvesting:**
Arjun, anxious about his exams, enters his student ID and password. The fake site displays "Verification successful – your registration is confirmed" to avoid raising suspicion.

**Step 4 – The Attack Executes:**
The attacker now has Arjun's login credentials. Within two hours, they access his university email account and send phishing emails to everyone in his contact list, continuing the chain. His personal details (name, reg. no., phone number) are compiled and sold on a dark web marketplace.

**Step 5 – Consequences:**
Arjun only discovers the breach three days later when a classmate alerts him. He is unable to access his email account. His personal data is now in the hands of unknown third parties, and his university account requires a full security reset. Several of his contacts also report receiving suspicious emails appearing to come from him.

---

## Who Is Typically Targeted?

Students, newly joined employees, and elderly users are the most common phishing targets because they may be less familiar with security red flags and are more likely to act on urgency-based messages.
