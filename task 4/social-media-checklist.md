# Social Media Do's and Don'ts for College Students
## Digital Literacy Project – Task 4 | CSE0001 | VIT Bhopal University

---

## ✅ DO's

1. **Keep your profiles professional** – Use a clear, appropriate profile photo and a real name on LinkedIn and GitHub. Future employers will search for you.
2. **Think before you post** – Ask yourself: Would I be comfortable if my professor, parent, or future employer saw this? If not, don't post it.
3. **Verify before sharing** – Always check whether a news article, statistic, or image is from a credible source before sharing or retweeting it.
4. **Use privacy settings** – Review and adjust who can see your posts, photos, and personal information on every platform you use.
5. **Keep your college email separate** – Use your college email for academic platforms (GitHub, LinkedIn, Kaggle) and a separate personal email for casual signups.
6. **Credit original creators** – If you share someone's content, tag or mention the original creator. This is both ethical and professional.
7. **Log out from shared devices** – Always log out of all social media accounts when using college lab computers or shared devices.

---

## ❌ DON'Ts

1. **Don't share your real-time location** – Never post "I'm at [location] right now" with live check-ins. This is a safety risk.
2. **Don't use social media to vent about college, professors, or classmates** – Even a deleted post can be screenshotted. This can result in disciplinary action.
3. **Don't click suspicious links in DMs** – Even if the message appears to come from a friend's account, do not click unknown links. Their account may have been compromised.
4. **Don't share personal documents publicly** – Never post photos of your ID card, hall ticket, fee receipt, or Aadhaar card on any platform.
5. **Don't engage with trolls or hate speech** – Responding to provocative or abusive comments escalates the situation. Use the Report and Block features instead.
6. **Don't use the same password across platforms** – If one account is compromised, all your accounts become vulnerable.
7. **Don't accept friend/follow requests from strangers** – Especially on platforms where you share personal updates. Verify who you are connecting with.
